# Automate Real-World Asset Tokenization with Our New Centrifuge Node for n8n

We're excited to announce the release of our latest community node for n8n: n8n-nodes-centrifuge. As automation specialists at Velocity BPA, we're constantly expanding the n8n ecosystem to support emerging technologies, and today we're bridging the gap between workflow automation and decentralized finance.

## The Challenge of RWA Integration

Real-World Asset (RWA) tokenization is transforming how businesses interact with financial instruments, but integrating Centrifuge's powerful platform into existing workflows has traditionally required custom development and blockchain expertise. Whether you're managing investment pools, tracking loan originations, or monitoring tokenized assets, manual processes create bottlenecks and increase the risk of errors.

## Introducing n8n-nodes-centrifuge

Our new Centrifuge node brings RWA tokenization capabilities directly into your n8n workflows. Now you can automate pool management, track investments, monitor loan statuses, and interact with tokenized real-world assets without writing a single line of code.

Key features include:
- **Pool Management**: Automate the creation, monitoring, and management of Centrifuge pools
- **Investment Tracking**: Monitor investments and redemptions in real-time
- **Loan Operations**: Streamline loan origination, tracking, and reporting
- **Token Management**: Handle RWA tokenization workflows seamlessly
- **Integration Ready**: Connect Centrifuge data with your existing tools like Slack, databases, CRMs, and more

## Getting Started

Installing the Centrifuge node is straightforward. In your n8n instance, simply run:


npm install n8n-nodes-centrifuge


After installation, restart n8n and you'll find the Centrifuge node available in your node palette, ready to integrate into your workflows.
